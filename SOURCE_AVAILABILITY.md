# Source availability and licensing

SmartCorners is a free binary download, not an open-source release. Its application source is not publicly available, and no first-party open-source license is granted by this repository.

SmartCorners includes the Sparkle update framework. Its license is available in [Sparkle-LICENSE.txt](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES/Sparkle-LICENSE.txt) and in the app bundle at `Contents/Resources/Sparkle-LICENSE.txt`.
